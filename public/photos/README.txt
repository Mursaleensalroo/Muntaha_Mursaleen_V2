DROP YOUR PHOTOS HERE
=====================

Just upload your photos. Any names. The site reads this folder
automatically and shows every photo it finds.

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .avif, .gif

That's it. Upload, commit, and Vercel will rebuild your site
with the new photos in about a minute.

Tips:
  - Photos are shown in alphabetical order. If you want to control
    the order, prefix filenames with numbers (e.g. 01-firstdate.jpg,
    02-engagement.jpg).
  - Photos under 2MB load faster. Next.js will optimize them on serve,
    but starting smaller is always better.
  - Files starting with "." or "_" are ignored (useful for hidden files).
